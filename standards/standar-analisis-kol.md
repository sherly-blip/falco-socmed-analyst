# Standar Analisis Performa KOL/Affiliate Campaign

## Kapan Dipakai
- Post KOL campaign review
- Mid-campaign optimization (multi-wave)
- Evaluasi siapa yang di-rehire vs drop
- Affiliate program performance review

## Metrics Wajib Dianalisis

### Per KOL
| Metric | Definisi | Benchmark |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| Reach/Views | Jangkauan konten KOL | Dibanding rata-rata views organic KOL tsb |
| ER | Engagement rate konten campaign | Dibanding ER organic KOL tsb — kalau turun drastis = content tidak resonance |
| Comments quality | Sentiment dan isi komentar | Buying signals vs generic comments |
| Link clicks | Klik ke link brand | Dibanding KOL lain di campaign |
| Promo code usage | Penggunaan kode promo | Attribution langsung ke KOL |
| Sales attributed | Penjualan yang ter-atribusi | ROI ultimate |

### Cost Efficiency Per KOL
| Metric | Formula | Kenapa Penting |
|--------|---------|---------------|
| CPR | Cost / Reach | Efisiensi jangkauan |
| CPE | Cost / Total Engagement | Efisiensi engagement |
| CPC | Cost / Link Clicks | Efisiensi traffic |
| CPA | Cost / Conversions | Efisiensi conversion |

### Aggregate Campaign
| Metric | Level |
|--------|-------|
| Total reach | Campaign-wide |
| Total engagement | Campaign-wide |
| Total conversions/sales | Campaign-wide |
| Overall ROAS | Campaign-wide |
| Average CPA | Campaign-wide |

## Alur Analisis KOL

### Overview
- Total KOL yang terlibat, tier distribution
- Total spend vs total result
- Overall campaign ROAS
- Comparison dengan campaign KOL sebelumnya (kalau ada)

### Detail Per KOL
- Profil: tier, niche, followers, platform
- Content: format, eksekusi, brief compliance
- Metrics: semua metrics + kontekstualisasi
- Audience response: sentiment komentar, buying signals

### Ranking
- Top KOL by reach (siapa yang paling luas)
- Top KOL by engagement (siapa yang paling engage)
- Top KOL by conversion (siapa yang paling convert)
- Top KOL by cost efficiency (siapa yang paling efisien per rupiah)
- Worst performers + reasoning
- Note: Top by reach ≠ top by conversion — ini insight krusial

### Pattern Analysis
- Pattern tier: micro vs macro vs nano — tier mana yang perform?
- Pattern format: review vs tutorial vs storytelling — format mana?
- Pattern brief compliance: KOL yang deviate vs yang patuh — siapa yang perform?
- Pattern audience: buying signals dari audience KOL mana?
- Pattern niche: niche mana yang paling resonance?
- Cross-pattern: kombinasi tier + format + niche yang optimal

### Kesimpulan
- KOL profile yang perform vs tidak perform
- Brief compliance vs creative freedom — mana yang lebih baik?
- Tier efficiency: tier mana yang paling efisien per rupiah
- Content format winning formula untuk campaign KOL

### Saran
- Rehire list: siapa yang perlu di-rehire + reasoning berbasis data
- Drop list: siapa yang tidak perlu di-rehire + reasoning
- Brief optimization: apa yang perlu diubah di brief berdasarkan pattern
- Tier allocation: bagaimana alokasi budget per tier ke depan
- Format direction: format konten apa yang harus di-brief kan

## Khusus Affiliate Analysis
- Aktif vs inactive rate
- Top affiliate performers
- Sales distribution: apakah concentrated di few affiliates atau spread?
- Commission efficiency
- Saran: bagaimana meningkatkan active rate dan per-affiliate performance
